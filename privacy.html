04

Statutory Rights under the DPDP Act (India, 2023)

Our data governance strictly adheres to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of the Republic of India. As a prospective homeowner or patron, you retain explicit statutory rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a complete summary of all personal information and communication notes held in our atelier records.
  • Right to Rectification: You may update or correct any obsolete contact numbers, mailing addresses, or representative designations at any time.
  • Right to Erasure: You may instruct our data steward to permanently purge your personal records from our systems, subject only to mandatory statutory disclosures required by HP RERA for executed allotment agreements.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Direct recourse is available through our dedicated Data Protection Officer at privacy@auracrest.in.
05

Architectural Folios & Mutual Discretion

Because Aura Crest comprises only twenty-five bespoke residences, our technical architectural drawings, structural calculations for Seismic Zone V, and floor plan allocations carry substantial creative and operational value.

When our atelier releases high-resolution CAD schematics, bespoke terrace engineering folios, or pricing allocations to prospective purchasers, we do so under mutual trust and non-disclosure. We safeguard your identity and investment discussions with identical bilateral discretion.

06

Digital Infrastructure & Minimal Cookies

Our digital portal has been engineered for effortless visual clarity, responsive performance, and high-altitude photography presentation. We maintain an uncompromising stance against digital clutter:

  • Essential Session State Only: We use minimal, functional first-party cookies necessary to maintain site stability, keep track of form completion state, and retain your viewing preferences.
  • Zero Commercial Trackers: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-domain behavioral tracking networks, or commercial social retargeting scripts.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your browser and our server are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
