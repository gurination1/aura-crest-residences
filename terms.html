02

Section 118 Compliance (HP Tenancy & Land Reforms Act)

Property transactions in Himachal Pradesh are governed by specific statutory protections. Under Section 118 of the Himachal Pradesh Tenancy and Land Reforms Act, 1972, non-agricultural individuals and buyers who do not originate from Himachal Pradesh require statutory permission from the State Government to purchase built-up residential property.

Atelier Conveyancing Guidance

The legal and conveyancing counsel at Aura Crest provides structured, step-by-step facilitation for every prospective purchaser requiring Section 118 clearance. From initial petition submission to State Cabinet clearance and final registered deed execution, our desk ensures full procedural compliance with zero ambiguity.

We execute formal agreements only when all prerequisite permissions and transparent title verifications are in place.

For guest safety and mountain logistics, all site inspections must be scheduled in advance with our resident concierge desk (+91 90411 72159). Unscheduled or unaccompanied access to active construction or terrace zones is strictly prohibited.

06

Seismic Zone V Engineering & High-Altitude Integrity

Kangra Valley is classified under Seismic Zone V. Aura Crest has been structurally engineered to exceed Indian Standard codes (IS 1893 and IS 13920) for severe seismic conditions.

Our foundations integrate deep mountain bedrock anchoring, ductile reinforced structural concrete framing, snow-load engineered roof pitches, and high-tensile thermal glazing tested for sub-zero Himalayan winters. Certified engineering drawings and vetting certifications are available for review during in-person atelier appointments.
